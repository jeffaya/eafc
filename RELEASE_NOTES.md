# Release Notes

## V18.0
- EA rating becomes the anchor of Impact.
- Impact = 70% EA + 30% position-aware statistical score.
- Difference from EA is capped at ±1.5.
- Keeps position-aware creation, passing, tackles and goalkeeper statistics.
- Ranking remains by Impact.
- Keeps V17.1 replay fallback.
- Does not ship/reset state, history or real club configuration.
